Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our safety standards, downtime, and physician-led aesthetic treatments.

Results depend on the procedure. Dermal fillers provide immediate volume and contour improvement that settles smoothly within 7 to 10 days. Neurotoxins (Botox) typically begin showing results within 3 to 5 days, reaching peak efficacy at day 14. For fractional laser resurfacing, mild redness resembling a light sunburn lasts 24 to 48 hours, with progressive collagen remodeling visible over the next 6 to 12 weeks. Most clients resume everyday social activities immediately or the following day.

At Lumineau Aesthetics, natural, undetectable refinement is our core medical philosophy. We strictly practice micro-dosing and respect native facial anatomy to preserve your full range of emotional expression. Our goal is for people to notice your vibrant, refreshed glow—never that you have had aesthetic work done.

All medical injectables and advanced laser procedures are administered exclusively by board-certified dermatologists and licensed, master-trained registered nurse injectors with thousands of documented procedures. We never delegate clinical injections to unsupervised assistants or technicians.

Patient comfort is paramount. We formulate medical-grade prescription topical numbing creams applied prior to procedure. Our dermal fillers contain integrated lidocaine, and our laser platforms feature continuous chilled-air skin cooling technology. Most patients describe treatments as a minor pinch or gentle warmth.

To minimize bruising, we advise avoiding alcohol, ibuprofen, aspirin, and fish oil supplements for 48 hours prior to your visit. Arrive well-hydrated and without makeup if possible. During your consultation, we review your full health profile and discuss any current skincare products you are using.
