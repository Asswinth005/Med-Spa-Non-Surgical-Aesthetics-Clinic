Contact FAQs

Quick answers to help you prepare for your visit or inquiry.

Our team responds to all form submissions within 24 hours on business days. If you submit on a weekend or holiday, you'll hear from us on the next business day. For urgent matters, please call us directly at (555) 727-4SPA.

Yes — we recommend arriving 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time. This allows you to complete any necessary paperwork, settle in, and have a brief consultation with your provider without feeling rushed. First-time patients should arrive 20 minutes early.

Same-day appointments are sometimes available depending on our schedule and the treatment requested. We recommend calling us directly at (555) 727-4SPA to check availability. Prestige members have same-day priority booking as part of their membership benefits.

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance may be subject to a fee equal to 50% of the scheduled service. We understand emergencies happen — please just communicate with us.
