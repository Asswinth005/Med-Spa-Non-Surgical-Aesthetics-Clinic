How it works

Botulinum toxin temporarily relaxes the tiny facial muscles that create dynamic wrinkles. By precisely targeting specific muscles, we soften lines while preserving your natural expressions. Our physicians use a micro-dosing approach — smaller amounts placed with anatomical precision — for results that look incredibly natural.

Laser resurfacing

Fractional laser technology that stimulates collagen, improves texture, and reverses sun damage — for skin that looks visibly younger.

Book Laser

What it treats

Sun damage Fine lines Acne scarring Enlarged pores Uneven texture Pigmentation Melasma Surgical scars

How it works

Our fractional laser creates microscopic columns of thermal energy in the deeper layers of skin. This controlled injury triggers your body's natural healing response — producing fresh collagen, elastin, and new skin cells. Over the following weeks and months, treated areas become smoother, firmer, and more even in tone.

The Lumineau difference

  • FDA-cleared platforms: We invest in the latest fractional and fully ablative laser technology.
  • Custom protocols: Settings are tailored to your skin type, concern, and downtime preference.
  • Comfort cooling: Integrated chilled-air systems keep you comfortable throughout.

Treatment Questions

Botox & Laser FAQs

Answers to the most common questions about our injectable and laser treatments.

Botox has been FDA-approved for cosmetic use since 2002 and has an excellent safety profile. Common side effects are mild and temporary: slight redness, swelling, or bruising at injection sites. Rarely, a temporary eyelid droop or asymmetry can occur, but this resolves on its own within weeks. Our physicians use precise anatomical knowledge to minimize these risks.

Avoid sun exposure and tanning for at least 2 weeks before your treatment. Discontinue retinoids and exfoliating acids 3–5 days prior. Arrive with clean skin (no makeup, lotions, or perfumes). If you have a history of cold sores, let us know — we may prescribe an antiviral. We'll provide detailed aftercare instructions including sun protection and gentle skincare.

Laser resurfacing can enhance the penetration of topical products, and Botox works on muscles while laser works on skin texture — together they address different aspects of aging. For most concerns, we recommend a series of 3–5 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance treatments 1–2 times per year. Superficial concerns like mild sun damage may improve with 1–2 sessions, while deeper scarring or significant texture issues benefit from a full series. Your physician will give you a personalized estimate.

Preventive Botox (sometimes called "baby Botox") uses smaller doses in patients who don't yet have deep lines, aiming to stop wrinkles from forming. Corrective Botox uses slightly higher doses to soften lines that are already present. Both are effective — the best approach depends on your age, muscle strength, and aesthetic goals. We'll discuss which is right for you.
