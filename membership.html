Membership Questions

Membership FAQs

Everything you need to know about joining, using, and managing your membership.

Your membership discount is automatically applied to every treatment you receive. The percentage depends on your tier — 10% for Essential, 15% for Signature, and 25% for Prestige. Discounts apply to all injectables, lasers, facials, and body treatments. There's no cap on how much you can save.

Yes. You can pause your membership for up to 3 months per year, or cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel, you'll retain access to any unused complimentary treatments for 60 days.

Complimentary treatments are credited to your account annually on your membership anniversary. You can book them anytime during the year, subject to availability. Free Botox areas and laser sessions are subject to a brief consultation to ensure they're appropriate for your goals. Unused complimentary treatments expire 12 months after being credited.

Absolutely. You can upgrade at any time — your new benefits take effect immediately, and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Many members start with Essential and upgrade to Signature after experiencing the value.

Membership discounts typically cannot be combined with other promotional offers, as they already represent our best pricing. However, members do receive exclusive access to member-only promotions throughout the year, which are designed to stack with your regular membership discount.
